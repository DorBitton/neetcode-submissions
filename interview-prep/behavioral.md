# Behavioral Interview Questions

SRE/DevOps behavioral interviews focus on **incidents, reliability decisions, and cross-team collaboration**.

---

## The STAR Framework

Every behavioral answer follows the same structure:

```
Situation   → context, what was happening
Task        → what was your role / what needed to be done
Action      → specifically what YOU did (not "we")
Result      → measurable outcome, what you learned
```

Keep answers to 2-3 minutes. Lead with the result if it's strong.

---

## The 10 Questions You Will Get

Write your own answers below each one. Practice out loud.

**1. Tell me about a major incident you managed. What happened and how did you handle it?**

What they're looking for: structured incident response, communication under pressure, technical depth, blameless postmortem mindset.

Key points to hit:
- How you detected it (monitoring, alert, user report?)
- How you communicated (stakeholders, status page)
- How you diagnosed (what tools, what you ruled out)
- How you resolved it
- What you changed afterwards to prevent recurrence

**2. Describe a time you improved the reliability or performance of a system.**

What they're looking for: proactive SRE mindset, ability to measure impact, understanding of SLOs.

Key points to hit:
- What the baseline was (uptime, latency, error rate)
- What you identified as the problem
- What you changed
- Measurable improvement (X% reduction in errors, Y ms latency improvement)

What they're looking for: technical conviction, communication skills, ability to influence without authority, knowing when to escalate vs accept.

Key points to hit:

- How you raised it (data, not opinion)
